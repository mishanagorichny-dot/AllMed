Women’s Health

Specialised care for women at every stage of life, from first periods to menopause and beyond.

Book an appointment Call 01 572 2012

Care that listens first

AllMed provides specialised care for women at every stage of life. Our team takes a respectful, supportive and discreet approach to every consultation.

Whether it’s a question you’ve been putting off or ongoing support, you can start with an appointment and we’ll take it from there.

What we can help with

  • Menstrual health
  • Hormonal concerns
  • Menopause support
  • Breast health
  • Pelvic health

How we care for you

Respectful

You’re listened to, and your questions are taken seriously.

Supportive

Continuous care at every stage, not a one-off visit.

Discreet

Consultations are confidential, and your records are handled to ICGP guidelines.

Booking your appointment

  1. Book online or call

    Choose Women’s Health online, or call reception on 01 572 2012.

  2. Pick a time that suits you

    Weekdays from 8:00 to 19:00, or Saturday mornings from 8:00 to 14:00.

  3. Visit us at Royal Canal Park

    Unit 2, Meridian Court, Royal Canal Park, Dublin 15, D15 Y2FV.

Questions

Do I need to be registered with AllMed?

We’re currently accepting applications from new patients. Complete the registration form and our team will contact you to let you know if we have space. Join the practice

Is my consultation confidential?

Yes. We follow Medical Council guidelines and the ICGP Data Protection Guideline for GPs. Read our privacy statement

What about family planning or pregnancy care?

Contraceptive advice, fertility guidance, pregnancy planning and antenatal care are part of our Family Planning & Antenatal Care service. See the service
