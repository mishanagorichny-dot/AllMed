# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, multi-page, no build step (user's choice). Shared design tokens and components in CSS; small vanilla JS modules. Must stay portable to WordPress (the live site is WordPress).

## Users

- Patients and families in Dublin 15 (Blanchardstown, Castleknock, Clonsilla area) looking for a GP, or existing patients who need a practical task done: book, request a repeat prescription, find hours or directions, call.
- Prospective new patients checking whether the practice accepts new registrations ("Join the Practice").
- People seeking a specific non-GP service: physiotherapy, women's health, PRP, laser, anti-aging skin care, neurodiversity support.

Many arrive on a phone, often while unwell or stressed. The job is almost always "get to the right action fast".

## Product Purpose

The website for AllMed Clinic, a private general practice in Dublin 15. Success means a visitor understands within seconds what the clinic is, what it offers, where it is, and how to book, then does it with minimal friction.

## Positioning

A single Dublin 15 practice that combines everyday GP care with women's health, family planning and antenatal care, physiotherapy, neurodiversity support and medical aesthetics under one roof. It is open 8:00–19:00 on weekdays and on Saturday mornings.

## Product Principles

1. Action first: book, call, directions and hours are never more than one tap away.
2. Truth over polish: no invented claims, people, or numbers.
3. Calm under stress: plain language, generous type, and no pop-ups interrupting the first visit.
4. One clinic, many needs: GP care is the anchor, and the specialist services sit clearly around it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. The audience includes older patients, unwell visitors, and neurodivergent people (the practice offers neurodiversity support). That means readable type (≥17px body), clear focus states, respect for reduced motion, and no forced popups.
