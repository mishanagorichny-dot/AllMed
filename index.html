Services under one roof

Start with your GP, or come to us directly for women’s health, physiotherapy, screening and skin care.

Everyday care

  • Comprehensive primary healthcare for individuals and families of all ages. Our experienced GPs are your first point of contact for everyday medical concerns, providing accurate diagnoses, effective treatment plans, and ongoing health management in a compassionate and confidential environment.

    Book a GP appointment

  • Screening helps detect potential health issues before they become serious. We offer tailored screening packages to assess cardiovascular risk, diabetes, cancers and other chronic conditions, so you can act early.

    Book a screening

Women & family

  • Specialised care for women at every stage of life, covering menstrual health, hormonal concerns, menopause support, breast health and pelvic health, with a respectful, supportive and discreet approach.

    Book an appointment About women’s health

  • Contraceptive advice, fertility guidance and pregnancy planning for individuals and couples. During pregnancy, our antenatal care provides continuous monitoring, education and support for a healthy pregnancy.

    Book an appointment

Recovery & support

  • Personalised rehabilitation and injury prevention. Whether you’re recovering from surgery, managing chronic pain or improving mobility, our physiotherapists use evidence-based techniques to restore strength, function and confidence.

    Book physiotherapy

  • Inclusive, tailored support for neurodivergent people, including those with autism, ADHD and learning differences. We offer assessments, therapy and guidance in a respectful environment that focuses on strengths.

    Book an appointment

Skin & aesthetics

  • PRP uses concentrated platelets from your own blood to promote tissue regeneration. We use it to treat hair thinning, joint pain and skin ageing: a non-surgical option with minimal downtime.

    Book a consultation

  • Suprana laser technology for skin rejuvenation, pigmentation, acne scars and hair removal. A non-invasive treatment with minimal discomfort and downtime, delivered by our certified professionals.

    Book a consultation

  • Clinically backed anti-aging treatments tailored to your skin type and goals, from advanced facials and serums to injectable therapies that reduce fine lines and improve skin texture.

    Book a consultation

Good to know

Answers to the questions our reception team hears most.

Ask us something else
Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, we’re currently accepting applications. Complete the online registration form and a member of our team will contact you as soon as possible to let you know if we have space on our list. Submitting the form doesn’t guarantee acceptance.

Can I register my children?

Children can be included on a parent’s application. Adults need to complete their own separate form.

How do I request a repeat prescription?

Use our online prescription request for medication we’ve prescribed before. Please allow at least 48 hours, or 72 hours if you request on a Friday. Once approved, we send it to your nominated pharmacy.

New medication, changes or other clinical questions need a GP review. Request a prescription

Why might I need a review or blood test first?

For your safety, some medication needs more frequent review or blood monitoring. This may mean a GP or nurse appointment before your prescription is renewed.

Can I get a sick certificate?

Sickness certificates are prepared by our team, then checked and signed by your GP. Call reception to arrange one.

How is my personal information handled?

We follow Medical Council guidelines and have signed up to the ICGP Data Protection Guideline for GPs. We only collect what’s needed for your care and keep it secure. Read our privacy statement
